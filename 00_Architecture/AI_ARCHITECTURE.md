## 1. Introduction
This document outlines the architecture for Artificial Intelligence (AI) components within the AITOS v2 system. It defines the scope, responsibilities, and operational principles of AI modules, ensuring their effective and responsible integration into the trading platform.

## 2. Purpose
The primary purpose of the AI Architecture is to:
*   **Clarify AI Roles:** Define where and how AI is utilized within the AITOS system.
*   **Ensure Responsible AI:** Establish guidelines for explainability, auditability, and human oversight.
*   **Standardize AI Development:** Provide a framework for the development, deployment, and maintenance of AI models.
*   **Maximize Alpha Generation:** Leverage AI to identify complex patterns, adapt to market changes, and enhance decision-making.

## 3. AI Boundaries and Responsibilities
AI components in AITOS are designed to augment, not replace, fundamental market understanding and human oversight. Their primary responsibilities include:
*   **Pattern Recognition:** Identifying complex, non-linear patterns in market data that are difficult for rule-based systems to detect.
*   **Adaptive Learning:** Adjusting strategies and parameters in response to changing market regimes.
*   **Predictive Analytics:** Forecasting market movements, volatility, or other relevant metrics.
*   **Supervisory Validation:** Providing a higher-level check on signals and decisions generated by other modules.
*   **Optimization:** Enhancing execution strategies, portfolio allocation, and risk management.

## 4. AI Lifecycle
The lifecycle of an AI model within AITOS follows a structured pipeline:

### Training Pipeline
*   **Data Collection:** Gathering relevant historical and real-time data from `02_Datasets`.
*   **Data Preprocessing:** Cleaning, normalizing, and transforming raw data into suitable formats.
*   **Model Selection:** Choosing appropriate AI algorithms (e.g., neural networks, reinforcement learning, ensemble methods).
*   **Hyperparameter Tuning:** Optimizing model parameters for best performance.
*   **Validation:** Rigorous testing against unseen data to prevent overfitting and ensure generalization.

### Feature Engineering
*   **Definition:** The process of creating new input features for AI models from raw data to improve model performance and interpretability.
*   **Sources:** Features can be derived from `Candle`, `Tick`, `Market Structure`, `Liquidity Zone`, `Order Block`, `FVG`, and other domain-specific insights.
*   **Standardization:** Feature definitions and generation processes are documented and version-controlled.

### Model Lifecycle
*   **Development:** Building and training AI models in a controlled environment.
*   **Deployment:** Integrating trained models into the `05_Implementation` layer.
*   **Monitoring:** Continuously tracking model performance, drift, and data quality in production.
*   **Retraining/Adaptation:** Periodically retraining models with new data or adapting them to new market conditions.
*   **Archiving:** Storing historical versions of models for reproducibility and auditability.

## 5. AI Governance Principles
To ensure responsible, transparent, and effective use of AI within AITOS, the following governance principles are established:

### AI Responsibilities and Limitations
*   **Responsibilities:** Clearly define the specific tasks and decision-making scope for each AI component.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly document the boundaries, assumptions, and known failure modes of AI models. AI should augment deterministic systems, not replace them without clear justification.

### Model Versioning
*   **Requirement:** All AI models must be version-controlled, with each version linked to its training data, features, and evaluation metrics.
*   **Mechanism:** Implement a robust model registry that tracks model lineage and allows for rollback to previous versions.

### Dataset and Feature Lineage
*   **Requirement:** Maintain clear lineage for all datasets and features used in AI model training and inference.
*   **Mechanism:** Document the origin, transformations, and versions of data and features to ensure reproducibility and auditability.

### Model Evaluation
*   **Requirement:** AI models must undergo continuous and rigorous evaluation against predefined performance metrics and business objectives.
*   **Mechanism:** Establish automated evaluation pipelines, including backtesting, forward testing, and A/B testing, with results documented in `04_Tests/`.

### Confidence Scoring
*   **Requirement:** AI models must output a confidence score alongside their predictions or decisions.
*   **Purpose:** Allows downstream modules (e.g., `AITOS-RISK`) to weigh AI recommendations appropriately and enables human operators to understand the certainty of AI outputs.

### Explainability
*   **Requirement:** All AI models must provide a degree of explainability, allowing for understanding of *why* a particular decision was made.
*   **Techniques:** Use of interpretable models, SHAP/LIME values, feature importance analysis, and decision trees.
*   **Integration:** Explainability insights are integrated into the `TRACEABILITY.md` and `Journal` for auditing.

### Human/Rule Override
*   **Requirement:** The system must always allow for human intervention and override of AI-generated decisions.
*   **Mechanism:** Clear protocols and interfaces for human operators to review, approve, or reject AI recommendations, especially in high-risk or anomalous situations.
*   **Logging:** All human overrides are meticulously logged and analyzed for future model improvements.

### Model Approval
*   **Requirement:** All AI models intended for production deployment must undergo a formal approval process.
*   **Mechanism:** Define a clear workflow for model validation, risk assessment, and stakeholder sign-off before deployment, adhering to `QUALITY_GATE.md`.

## 6. Conclusion
The AITOS AI Architecture provides a robust and ethical framework for integrating artificial intelligence into the trading platform. By focusing on clear boundaries, a structured lifecycle, and core principles like explainability and human oversight, AITOS leverages AI to enhance performance while maintaining control and transparency.
